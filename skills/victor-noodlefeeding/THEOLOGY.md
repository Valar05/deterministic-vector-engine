# Victor Noodlefeeding — Theology

This is the deepest doctrine layer beneath the ordinary work surface.

Users should not need to enter this layer to make art. Fortress maintains it so artifacts, factories, and foundries remain coherent as techniques are excavated, mutated, recombined, and compiled into different final forms.

## Dependency

**THEOLOGY -> FOUNDRY -> FACTORY -> ARTIFACT**

- **Artifact:** the usable thing.
- **Factory:** the reproducible way to make or vary the thing.
- **Foundry:** the reusable grammar of parts, operators, transforms, materials, clips, masks, primitives, cages, projection and compilation targets used by factories.
- **Theology:** the selection laws that decide what survives and why.

## Articles

1. **Perception outranks representation.** The final form is whichever representation preserves the intended read with the least computational and memory cost.
2. **Simple causes may produce dense effects.** Prefer composition, transformation, timing, masking and reuse before geometric proliferation.
3. **Animation and modeling are sibling uses of transformation.** Played through time, a transform is animation; sampled or baked, it is modeling.
4. **Factories compound.** A useful artifact is good; a reusable factory is better; a foundry that breeds factories is better still.
5. **Fertile failure is evidence.** An unintended result that creates a coherent visual language should be preserved rather than erased.
6. **The artist should not pay the machinery tax.** Lower layers own complexity and expose the smallest useful controls upward.
7. **The potato is a judge.** Source purity, elegance and geometric correctness do not excuse a representation that cannot respond on weak hardware.
8. **Meaning survives migration.** Vector, sprite, mesh, primitive assembly, cached raster, atlas or another format may all be temporary bodies for the same authored intent.
9. **Every thing has a place.** Nothing becomes durable canon until its artifact, factory, foundry dependencies, and governing doctrine can be located.
10. **Make the eye believe more than the machine had to compute.** This is the operational center of Victor Noodlefeeding.

## Liturgical compression
