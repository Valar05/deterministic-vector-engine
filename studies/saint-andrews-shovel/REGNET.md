# Regnet wound ledger — Saint Andrew's Shovel

Stopping authority: Drew's cognition. Machine checks can only prevent known regressions.

## Crossed streams

The current vector study does some things better than the raster oracle: it has cleaner silhouette ownership, stronger thumbnail legibility, explicit open D-handle topology, and a line hierarchy that still describes the object when color is mentally removed.

The raster oracle does the harder half better: material truth. Its wood, gunmetal, bright steel, dents, grime, edge wear, and reflections are continuous surface phenomena rather than regions of flat color bounded by ink. The blade feels thick and used; the shaft feels handled; the socket feels manufactured; black is material/value, not costume.

The desired child is not the average of the parents. Line art owns only the facts that need jurisdiction: outer contour, overlap, manufactured transitions, a few material seams, and selected damage. Gradients, deterministic SVG lighting, roughness, and topology-aware wear own the rest. Color multiplies the line system instead of rescuing it.

## Wounds

- H01 GRAIN_ESCAPE — D-handle grain paths escaped the wood grip and produced the two visible hairs. FIXED by material clipping, not erasure paint.
- S01 CEL_BORDER — heavy near-uniform outer/internal stroke made material planes read as cel shading. REDUCED by separating silhouette weight from internal material edges.
- M01 FLAT_METAL — blade and hardware used gradients but lacked roughness/specular breakup. ADDRESSED with deterministic turbulence + specular SVG filters.
- M02 FLAT_WOOD — shaft read as colored vector fill with grain lines laid on top. ADDRESSED with fixed-seed roughness/specular response while keeping authored grain.
- B01 LIVERY_BANDS — shaft black regions were too clean and therefore read as graphic costume. ADDRESSED by irregular deterministic wear/chipping at their boundaries.
- W01 DECORATIVE_SCRATCH — some scratches float independent of stress/topology. OPEN. Future passes should migrate wear toward edges, contact zones, socket transitions, and repeated handling regions.
- V01 BLADE_DEPTH — raster oracle has stronger thickness/reflection hierarchy at blade shoulder, keel, and bright lower-right steel. PARTIALLY ADDRESSED with edge glints and material shaders; still subject to Drew's visual verdict.
- H02 HARDWARE_DEPTH — collars/rivets remain simpler than oracle. OPEN until cognitive match.
