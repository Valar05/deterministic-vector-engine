# DVE Editorial Contour v1

- Canvas: transparent 800 x 1000 SVG.
- Ink: `#171717` only; every drawn element has `fill="none"`.
- Weights: 8 outer silhouette, 5 identity features, 3 texture/details.
- Geometry: manually authored `path` elements using only M/L/C/Q/Z, round caps and joins.
- Contours: broken where negative space carries the form. No shading, hatching, gradients, opacity, filters, masks, patterns, raster images, filled pupils, shadows, or color areas.
- Portrait policy: preserve identity anchors while correcting camera distortion, clutter, accidental asymmetry, and grooming noise. Appeal means stronger intentionality, not generic youth or erased age.
- No-tracing policy: the private reference may inform artistic proportions and landmark judgment only. Pixel sampling, automatic tracing, overlays, registered alignment, copied silhouettes, coordinate extraction, embedded images, and raster drawing inputs are prohibited.
