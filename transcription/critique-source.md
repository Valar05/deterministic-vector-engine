# Source critique before drawing

Observed without overlay or tracing: close wide-angle selfie; thick dark brows, brown eyes, broad nose, moustache, very full long beard, and irregular brown/gray curls. The source has strong identity but low presentation intentionality from lens distortion, tired-neutral expression, asymmetric flyaways, beard-edge noise, abrupt shoulder crop, and a cluttered domestic background. The portrait should preserve the mature rugged identity while correcting presentation rather than making the subject generically young.
